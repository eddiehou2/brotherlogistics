Who We Are

A full-service logistics company serving businesses and individuals throughout the Greater Toronto Area and nationwide.

Founded in Mississauga, Ontario, Brother Logistics has built a reputation for dependable, transparent shipping services. Whether you are sending a single parcel across town or managing recurring freight for your business, our team handles every shipment with the same level of care and precision.

We operate from a modern distribution hub near Pearson International Airport, giving us direct access to major air and ground carriers. Our routing technology and carrier partnerships allow us to offer competitive transit times without compromising on package security or tracking visibility.

From e-commerce fulfilment to corporate freight, we tailor solutions to your volume, timeline, and budget. Every shipment includes real-time tracking, proof of delivery, and dedicated customer support.

Our Services

End-to-end logistics solutions designed around how you ship.

  • Standard Ground Shipping Economical delivery for non-urgent parcels within Canada. Typical transit: 3–7 business days.
  • Express Courier Priority handling with next-day and two-day options available in major metropolitan areas.
  • International Shipping Customs documentation, duties guidance, and delivery to the United States and select global destinations.
  • Bulk & Pallet Freight LTL and full-truckload options for heavier shipments. Ideal for warehouses and manufacturing clients.
  • Scheduled Pickups Recurring daily or weekly pickups from your office or warehouse — no trip to a drop-off centre required.
  • Packaging & Labelling On-site packaging services and compliant labelling to reduce damage and customs delays.
